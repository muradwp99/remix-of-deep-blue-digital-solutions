# Product

## Register

brand

## Users

Founders, product leaders, and marketing heads at startups through mid-market companies evaluating a design + engineering studio for websites, web apps, mobile apps, and SaaS builds. They arrive skeptical, compare 3–5 agencies, and decide on perceived craft: the site itself is the portfolio. Secondary audience: senior designers/engineers assessing Northline as an employer (careers).

## Product Purpose

Northline's marketing site. Its one job is conversion: make a visitor believe "these people ship premium work" within 10 seconds, then move them to Start a Project / book a discovery call. Every page must demonstrate the craft it sells — motion, typography, and interaction quality ARE the sales pitch. Success = contact-form submissions and discovery calls booked.

## Brand Personality

Precise, cinematic, quietly confident. A studio, not a factory: senior, opinionated, no filler. Motion is choreographed and physical (masked reveals, scroll-scrubbed lines, parallax depth) — never bouncy, never decorative for its own sake. Copy is short, concrete, outcome-led ("3.1× signups", "62% faster"), allergic to agency clichés ("we craft digital experiences").

## Anti-references

- Generic SaaS-template landing pages: identical icon-card grids, hero-metric blocks, purple gradients.
- Cheap agency sites: stock handshake photos, "passionate about pixels" copy, bouncy easing.
- Over-animated award-bait that hurts readability or scroll performance; anything below 60fps.
- Light, airy, pastel startup aesthetics — this brand is dark, dense, and cinematic.

## Design Principles

1. **The site is the portfolio.** Every interaction must survive a designer's scrutiny; demo the service through the medium.
2. **Motion with a spine.** One physical system (masked reveals, blur-dissolves, scrub lines, magnetic pulls) applied deliberately per section — not one uniform entrance everywhere. Transform/opacity only in scrubbed paths; 120fps is the bar.
3. **Proof over adjectives.** Numbers, named case studies, concrete deliverables. Cut any sentence that could appear on a competitor's site.
4. **Conversion is choreography.** Each page ends in a next step; CTAs earn attention through contrast and placement, not size.
5. **Committed dark navy.** Near-black navy surfaces, one gold/lime accent system, gold reserved for warmth/emphasis. No new hues.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

WCAG AA. Full `prefers-reduced-motion` alternatives for every animation (crossfade or static). Body text ≥4.5:1 on navy. Keyboard-reachable nav/mega-menu. `aria-label` preserved on split-text headings.
